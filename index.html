Target Face Options

Single spot · 5-spot

›
Single Spot
One 40 cm face per archer. Scores 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 from centre out. The lower rings (1–3) score but are rarely used at competition level.
5-Spot
Five 16 cm mini-faces on a black background. Only 5 and 4 scoring zones — no 1/2/3 rings. One arrow per spot. Prevents Robin Hoods.
Archer chooses their face type before the round begins and cannot switch mid-round. The 5-spot is popular with compound shooters to avoid arrow damage.
